Install the Mini App

Your membership is ready. To protect the member experience, mobile access continues from the installed app.

After installation, open CLASSIC SAN SEBASTIAN CLUB from your phone's Home Screen. The dashboard will unlock automatically there.

iPhone / iPad

1. Open this page in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
4. Tap Add.
5. Close Safari and open the new San Sebastian icon from your Home Screen.

Android

If the install window does not appear automatically, open your browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. Then open the new icon from your Home Screen.
